 Series Overview

**JoJo's Bizarre Adventure* is a legendary manga and anime series created by **Hirohiko Araki*. It follows the multi-generational tales of the Joestar family.

Key Characteristics
* Epic chronological parts spanning over a century.
* Unique supernatural powers (*Hamon* and *Stands*).
* Distinctive art style and memorable character poses.
